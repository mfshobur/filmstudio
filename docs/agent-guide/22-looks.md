# 22 Looks: style guides for your scenes

Generated by `tools/gen-looks-doc.js` from `engine/plooks.js`. A look is **not a subject and not a ready-made scene**. It is a style guide in code: a palette, a background, effects and a motion feel. You draw the subjects (a rabbit, a robot, a city) in a `scene` file, and the look makes them belong to one visual world.

Use one with `scene({ look: "wayang", draw(c) { ... } })`, or per shot with `{"look": "ink"}` in the shot params. Inside `draw(c)`: `c.pal` holds the look's colours, `c.look` the whole look. The look paints its background first and applies its effects after you draw. Pass `bg: false` in `scene({...})` to paint your own background instead. List them with `filmstudio look list`, inspect one with `filmstudio look show <name>`, make your own with `filmstudio look new <name>` (a file in `looks/`).

Most looks follow a built-in style of the same name (`filmstudio styles`). The built-in styles are ready-made scenes you can pick as they are; the looks are what they were made from, so a custom scene in the same look matches them.
